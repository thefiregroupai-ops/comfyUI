Free Photorealistic & Skin Realism ComfyUI Workflows

Ultra-realistic skin texture and micro-detail enhancement workflows powered by Qwen and SeedVR2.

Choose refinement or upscaling based on the defect

Use Qwen AI Real Skin v5 when the portrait needs a staged skin and face refinement. Its graph is divided into base upscale, heavy refinement, and final SeedVR2 output, with controls for detail level, smoothness or softness, face-mask blur, and upscale amount. The multiple comparison and save nodes let you identify which stage improves the image and which stage begins to alter identity.

Use the SeedVR2 High-Resolution AI Upscaler when the source already looks correct and the main requirement is a larger, detailed image. That graph centers on image scaling, SeedVR2 processing, sharpening input, comparison, and saving rather than a dedicated face-mask refinement sequence.

Begin with the approved source, not an early draft. Inspect facial geometry, skin boundaries, hair, hands, clothing, and background texture before processing; enlargement can make an existing error more visible. Run one workflow at its saved baseline, compare source and output at the same zoom, and change only one exposed control per test. If Real Skin changes the person, compare its intermediate outputs and stop before the drifting stage. If the general upscaler invents texture or halos edges, return to its scaling or sharpening controls rather than stacking another pass.
